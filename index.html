Monthly retainer

Best when priorities change week to week. Time is tracked against agreed tasks, status is shared regularly, and invoices are issued every two weeks with Net 15 payment terms.

Working agreement essentials

Scope & acceptance

The signed statement of work names deliverables, file formats, milestones, review dates, and what counts as approval.

Revisions

Fixed projects include two review rounds unless the proposal says otherwise. New directions are quoted as a change request.

Payment

Projects use milestone billing; retainers are prepaid monthly; hourly support is invoiced every two weeks on Net 15 terms.

Ownership

Final approved deliverables transfer after full payment. Working files and third-party licenses are listed in the proposal.

Confidentiality

Client files and nonpublic information stay confidential and are used only to complete the agreed work.

Pause or exit

Either side may end a retainer with 30 days’ notice; completed work and approved expenses remain payable.

Pricing is a starting guide, not a binding quote. CAD work covers drafting, modeling, and prototyping support—not licensed engineering or stamped drawings. Materials, printing, paid assets, software subscriptions, and API usage are separate when needed.
